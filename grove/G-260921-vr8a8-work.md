## Meaning

An outcome someone wants, written so that another person or agent can pick it
up: what will be true for whom, its bounds, observable acceptance, what it
depends on, and its next action. Work is a record (`G-NNN`) and moves through
proposed, active, review and accepted, or abandoned by an explicit human
decision; done is derived from its accepted candidate's delivery,
never written (schema 4, below).
Creating or editing work authorizes nothing; an assignment does.

Not a plan (how the outcome will be built), not an [attempt](G-260921-sth8q-attempt.md)
(one execution of it), and not a ticket for every step: small preparation lives
in the work's own body.

## Relationships

Questions block work; decisions constrain it. A plan or review names the work
it belongs to. Work is carried out by one or more attempts, each producing a
[candidate](G-260921-jatts-candidate.md).
