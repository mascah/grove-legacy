## Meaning

What a work record is, as Grove derives it from the record and the target
rather than from its status field alone: proposed, active, review (awaiting
judgment, or an acceptance that no longer applies), accepted (awaiting
delivery), done (the target tip's own copy of the record is accepted for the
same candidate, which only a delivery brings there), abandoned, or unknown
when the target cannot be read. A `done` status from schema 3 is shown as
that schema's claim.

Not the status, which is what the file says and what a person or `update`
wrote; not a proof of the delivery, which `integrate` makes once and an
audit repeats on request; not a policy's "standing" delegation, an older use
of the word for a policy given in advance.
