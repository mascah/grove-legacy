## Meaning

What a work record is, as Grove derives it from the record and Git rather
than from its status field alone: proposed, active, review (awaiting
judgment, or an acceptance that no longer applies), accepted (awaiting
delivery), done (its delivery to the configured target verified), abandoned,
or unknown with the reason it cannot be decided, such as a shallow clone or
a missing submitted tip. A `done` status from schema 3 is shown as that
schema's claim, never as verified.

Not the status, which is what the file says and what a person or `update`
wrote; not a policy's "standing" delegation, an older use of the word for a
policy given in advance.
