## Outcome

A developer unfamiliar with Grove adopts it in an existing repository,
delegates a bounded change, leaves, and returns to an understandable result.
They can answer a question, continue through another supported harness,
judge the evidence, and deliver accepted work through their repository's
chosen integration workflow without the author nearby to explain it.

Owner intent and selected direction: [G-260930-e8jj7](G-260930-e8jj7-build-a-portable-workflo.md), 2026-09-29.
The brief owns product direction. This record owns milestone acceptance,
membership and the final independent-adoption trial.

## Scope and constraints

- Exercise Claude Code and Codex, local-only delivery and one protected
  hosted PR path. GitHub is the proposed first hosted implementation;
  support claims must name the actually exercised host and environments.
- Configure harness/model/effort, checks, permissions, resource limits and
  delivery method with an understandable default path. Capability
  differences remain visible; elapsed time must not be described as a
  guaranteed monetary cap.
- Preserve durable work, decisions, questions, checkpoints, reviews and
  delivery evidence across fresh sessions. Native transcripts and raw
  process logs may remain local. Switching harnesses promises continuation
  from project context, not translation of a private conversation.
- Keep independent review separate from acceptance. Automated action needs
  explicit authority and applicable policy. A successful process or passing
  checks alone cannot close the work.
- Existing files, Git and core local operation remain usable. Any format
  change has a deliberate migration retaining identity and evidence.
- Parallel fan-out, delegated LLM approval, a universal workflow builder,
  public plugin SDK, scheduling, fleet management and a git-host service
  are outside this milestone. This is not a commercial launch commitment.

## Acceptance

| Moment | Observable result |
| --- | --- |
| Adopt | In an existing repository, the developer establishes project instructions, harness, checks, limits and delivery method. Unsupported or missing capabilities have a clear remedy. |
| Shape | A conversation produces an outcome and acceptance they can explain, with the authorization to execute distinct from a proposal. |
| Delegate | They start a bounded change without selecting worktree paths or session IDs, and can leave the terminal. |
| Return | They can explain what happened, what needs their attention, and what can happen next from Grove's current view. |
| Continue | A required human choice becomes a durable question; answering it permits continuation. A supported harness change preserves the mandate, checkpoint and evidence without the original conversation. |
| Judge | The result explains changed behavior, decisions, verification, independent findings and unresolved concerns, tied to the actual candidate. |
| Deliver | The local path and hosted path establish that the accepted result reached the configured destination, or clearly explain the remaining wait. Useful history and evidence survive cleanup. |

Also exercise interruption, failed verification, an unanswered question,
a changed candidate invalidating earlier approval, target movement, and
integration performed outside Grove. No unchanged wait starts duplicate work.

The final trial uses a few developers outside the author's coached projects
in their own repositories, with the supported paths covered across the
cohort. Proposed trial size: three developers. Record unaided completions,
author interventions, misunderstandings of authority/completion, time spent
recovering context, and whether each would continue using Grove. Repairs
that affect the journey are exercised again. A walkthrough by the author or
completion of every child record is insufficient evidence on its own.
The owner judges both the experience and the limits of the resulting claims.
